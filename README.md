# Nationalist Academy

**Independent educational and information initiative based in Karachi, Pakistan.**

Nationalist Academy develops and maintains digital learning resources, student-support tools, educational publishing projects, and practical information resources. Our work is intended to make learning support more accessible while encouraging accuracy, transparency, privacy, and academic integrity.

## Our principles

1. **Academic integrity:** We support learning, practice, feedback, and responsible research. Students remain responsible for their own submissions and academic decisions.
2. **Accuracy and corrections:** We aim to use reliable sources, identify uncertainty, and correct material errors when reported.
3. **Privacy:** We seek to collect only information needed to operate a service and explain relevant data practices on the applicable service.
4. **Transparency:** We distinguish educational information from professional advice, official institutional information, and guaranteed outcomes.
5. **Copyright respect:** We respect intellectual property and provide a channel for copyright concerns and takedown requests.
